Our Story

Started in a kitchen, grown with the same care

Buy Alchimia began as a handful of jars sold from a home kitchen in Kurunegala. Every product still passes through the same small team that started it — from the farmer we buy from directly, to the batch we jar by hand.

We believe "organic" should mean something specific: traceable sourcing, no synthetic shortcuts, and a producer you could actually call on the phone. That's the standard behind every item in the shop, now shipped from Sri Lanka to customers worldwide.
